GeForce RTX 20, 30 and 40 cards run FP16, BF16 and TF32 tensor math at half speed; gpu-unlock lifts that limit at boot. Image generation (ComfyUI) and unquantized models get about 30–55% faster; GGUF models gain little and token speed doesn't change. It installs a kernel module and adds an option to the boot line, so every change needs a reboot. gpu-unlock is a young community project, not made by NVIDIA or GPU Shepherd: use it at your own risk.
