Move to another disk

Move models or an app's data to another place, such as a bigger disk. GPU Shepherd stops the apps that use the folder (letting running work finish), copies it, checks every file arrived, points the apps at the new place and starts them again. The old copy stays until you delete it here, so you can move back. A newly attached disk is mounted for you (see Drives below); GPU Shepherd never formats one.
